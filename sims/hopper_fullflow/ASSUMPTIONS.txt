HOPPER FULLFLOW MODEL - EVERY ASSUMPTION
=========================================
Compiled 2026-10-26 from every file in sims/hopper_fullflow (and from
general_fluid_network/vehicle_sim.py, "Luca's sim", where numbers came from).

How to read this file
---------------------
  #EDIT   the value is an input in HOPPER_DATA.py that carries an #EDIT tag
          there (the ones already known to be assumptions). Lines tagged
          "#EDIT GUESS" in HOPPER_DATA.py were added during the 2026-10-26
          work for values nobody has supplied yet.
  (none)  an assumption baked into the code, a default, or a modelling
          choice. Most need no input to change, but they shape the answers.

  Source codes:
    vsim    copied from vehicle_sim.py (the old model)
    sheet   Hopper Fluid Components Mastersheet.xlsx
    info    Extra Component Info.txt (part numbers)
    user    value you supplied in chat
    mine    chosen while building this model (no outside source)
    FF      built into FullFlow / ThermoProp (the backend)

Each item: ID, assumption, value, source, where it lives.


=============================================================================
1. FLUIDS AND PROPERTIES
=============================================================================
F-1   Oxidizer is ThermoProp "LOX"; fuel is ThermoProp "RP-1"; pressurant is
      ThermoProp real-gas "Nitrogen". (user/mine; HOPPER_DATA FLUIDS)
F-2   RP-1 properties are ThermoProp's RP-1 (812 kg/m3 at 293 K), not the
      n-Dodecane surrogate (752 kg/m3) vehicle_sim used. Fuel-side dP is
      ~8 % higher than the old model for the same flow. (mine)
F-3   Liquids are incompressible: density, viscosity, vapour pressure and
      enthalpy are evaluated ONCE per tank at the tank SET pressure and the
      LOADED temperature, and held for the whole run. (mine;
      Hopper_Prop_System.liquid_properties)
F-4   The feed-line liquid has the tank liquid's properties all the way to
      the injector: no heating of LOX in the lines, no fuel temperature rise
      in the regen jacket, no density change anywhere in a feed. (mine)
F-5   All states are single phase: no cavitation, flashing or two-phase flow
      in any line, valve or injector (quality is tracked but never used).
      (mine)
F-6   Ambient pressure is a constant 14.8 psia for the whole run (no
      altitude change during a hop, no weather). (vsim; AMBIENT_PRESSURE)
F-7   Chamber gas properties come from ThermoProp chemical-equilibrium
      solves (adiabatic flame, reactants at their tank temperatures: LOX
      90 K, RP-1 293 K), stored on a Pc x MR grid (10-610 psia x 31 points,
      MR 0.5-4.0 x 36 points) and interpolated; the old model used
      RocketCEA "Kerosene". (mine; Hopper_Chamber_Map.py)
F-8   Off-grid points are extrapolated from the map (solver trial points
      just outside it). (mine; Map.from_hdf5 extrapolate=True)

2. PRESSURANT SUPPLY (COPVs)
=============================================================================
C-1   3 COPVs x 9.0 L = 27 L, lumped into ONE gas volume (no manifold
      volume, no line volume, no pressure difference between bottles).
      (P&ID; COPV_COUNT, COPV_UNIT_VOLUME)
C-2   Initial COPV pressure 4503.2 psia. This is vehicle_sim's fill DENSITY

C-3   Initial COPV temperature 293 K. (vsim; PRESSURANT_INITIAL_TEMPERATURE)

      COPV pressure late in the burn (conservative). (mine)
C-5   COPV gas is well mixed (one temperature, one pressure). (FF Volume)

3. TANKS
=============================================================================
T-1   LOX tank volume 20.0 L. Sheet says 19.88 L. (vsim; LOX_TANK.volume)
T-2   Fuel tank volume 16.0 L. Sheet says 15.18 L. (vsim; FUEL_TANK.volume)
T-3   Loads: 19.43 kg LOX at 90 K, 9.7 kg RP-1 at 293 K. (vsim)
T-4   Both tanks start at their set pressure, 500 psia (absolute, not

T-5   Bang-bang band: solenoid closes above set + 5 psi, opens below set -

T-6   Bang-bang solenoids start OPEN (bang_bang_initial_state default 1.0)
      even though the tank is already at set pressure; this causes a
      ~517-520 psia spike in the first step. (mine; Hopper_Elements.Tank)
T-7   The bang-bang controller samples tank pressure once per accepted time
      step (every 0.05 s) and switches instantly (no solenoid response
      time). The real flight-computer rate and valve timing are unknown.
      (mine; bang_bang_state)
T-8   #EDIT  LOX ullage collapse factor 2.0: COPV supplies the full
      pressurant flow, only 1/2.0 of it stays as ullage gas. Assumed value
      (user, 2026-10-27); Luca to supply conservative and Vespula-heritage
      values. Not from test data. (For reference, 2.1 reproduced the COPV
      draw of vehicle_sim's 500 W/m2K ullage heat transfer.)
      (user; LOX_TANK.collapse_factor)
T-9   #EDIT  Fuel collapse factor 1.1 (some collapse even at ambient RP-1).
      Assumed value (user, 2026-10-27), same source pending as T-8.
      (user; FUEL_TANK.collapse_factor)
T-10  Collapse factor is constant over the whole burn (real collapse is
      strongest early, when the cold liquid surface first sees warm gas).
      (mine)
T-11  The "collapsed" pressurant simply disappears: its mass is not added
      to the liquid and its energy is not tracked. (mine)
T-12  Ullage gas is well mixed (one temperature), real-gas N2 solved in
      pressure-enthalpy. No heat transfer with tank walls or the liquid
      other than through the collapse factor. No stratification. (mine)
T-13  No propellant boil-off or evaporation into the ullage, no N2
      dissolving into LOX, no ullage composition other than pure N2. (mine)
T-14  Tank walls are rigid (no volume change with pressure or temperature).
      (mine)
T-15  Liquid is isothermal at its loaded temperature for the whole burn
      (no heating of LOX by the ullage, walls or environment). (mine)
T-16  Hydrostatic head and vehicle acceleration are neglected: the tank
      outlet sees the ullage pressure exactly. (vsim)
T-17  Tank outflow is drawn from the bottom with no vortexing, no
      geysering, no gas ingestion near empty. (mine)

4. PRESSURANT LEGS (COPV -> tank ullage)
=============================================================================
P-1   #EDIT  Pressurant line length 1.0 m (each leg). (vsim; GasLine length)
P-2   Pressurant line ID 4.92 mm (1/4" x 0.028" wall). (vsim)
P-3   Tube roughness 1.5e-5 m for every tube in the model. (vsim;
      TUBE_ROUGHNESS)
P-4   A gas line is a lumped equivalent CdA, A / sqrt(1 + f L / D), using
      the FULLY ROUGH friction factor (no Reynolds-number dependence), with
      no volume, no inertia, no wave dynamics. (mine; GasLine inertia=False)
P-5   No fittings, bends, tees, filters or flex hoses in the pressurant
      legs except the listed elements (no K-losses). (mine)
P-6   OBB / FBB solenoid CdA 18.68 mm2 each. Sheet says OBB = 2x PLV34 =
      2.14 mm2 and FBB = 1x PLV34 = 1.07 mm2. (user)
P-7   #EDIT  OR-1 (LOX side) CdA 2.04 mm2 = 2 x 1.02 mm2. This is the CdA
      vehicle_sim gave its "Ox Bang-Bang". (vsim)
P-8   #EDIT  OR-2 (fuel side) CdA 1.02 mm2. (vsim)
P-9   #EDIT  PROCK / PRFCK check valve CdA 12.0 mm2 each. No Cv published
      for the Check-All BUDSSTF.125SS. (mine)
P-10  Check valves are a fixed CdA in the forward direction and block all
      reverse flow; their 1/8 psi cracking pressure is ignored. (mine)
P-11  All restrictions in one leg (line + solenoid + orifice + check) are
      combined into ONE equivalent area with FullFlow SeriesCdA
      (1/CdA^2 = sum 1/CdA_i^2) feeding ONE compressible orifice. SeriesCdA
      is an incompressible series rule; real gas legs choke at the
      smallest restriction (likely OR-1/OR-2) with pressure recovery
      between restrictions, which this does not capture. (mine)
P-12  The compressible orifice uses ideal-gas flow equations with the real
      gas's local R and gamma at the COPV. At 4500 psi N2 is ~10 % away
      from ideal, so flow at high COPV pressure is approximate. (FF
      CompressibleOrifice)
P-13  Pressurant legs are adiabatic: gas between restrictions keeps the
      upstream enthalpy (real-gas Joule-Thomson cooling is captured at the
      junction property lookups; no heat from tube walls). (mine)
P-14  Solenoids open and close instantly (no stroke time). (mine)

=============================================================================
L-1   #EDIT  LOX tube 3/8" OD x 0.028" wall (ID 8.10 mm). (vsim; LOX_TUBE_ID)
L-2   #EDIT  Fuel tube 1/4" OD x 0.028" wall (ID 4.93 mm). (vsim;

L-3   LOX tank -> OTV line 1.0 m. (vsim)
L-4   OTV -> LOX injector line 0.2032 m (8 in). (vsim)
L-5   Fuel tank -> FTV line 0.3048 m (1 ft). (vsim)
L-6   FTV -> regen line 0.2032 m (8 in). (vsim)
L-7   Every line is horizontal (height_change = 0, no gravity head along
      any line). (mine)
L-8   No fittings, tees, bends, flex hoses, filters or QD bodies in the
      feeds except the listed elements (no K-losses). The P&ID's flex
      hoses and the OFD/FQD tees are not in the flow path. (mine)
L-9   Lines are FullFlow DarcyWeisbach: incompressible, with flow inertia,
      friction from the Churchill correlation (laminar to turbulent),
      fully developed flow. (FF)
L-10  Lines hold no propellant mass: junctions between elements have zero
      volume and the line content is not tracked. Lines start full of
      liquid at t = 0 (no priming, no chill-down, no gas in the lines).
      (vsim/mine)
L-11  Line content is NOT counted in the tank load or the residuals, so the
      real propellant on board (tank + lines) is higher than modelled, and
      line hold-up is not subtracted from the reported residual. (mine)
L-12  Junction nodes below a closed throttle valve start at ambient
      pressure; the rest start at tank pressure. (mine)

8. ENGINE AND NOZZLE
=============================================================================
E-1   Target MR 1.8, held constant at every throttle level. (vsim)
E-2   Throat area 672.26 mm2, exit area 2355.6 mm2 (expansion ratio 3.504).
      (vsim)
E-3   c* efficiency ramps linearly 0.80 at 100 psia -> 0.85 at 300 psia,
      flat outside. Same numbers as vehicle_sim, but vehicle_sim's formula
      used max(1, ...) and actually held 0.85 below 300 psia; this model
      uses the clamped ramp (changed on request). Over the 1dof burn
      eta_c* = 0.815-0.832. (vsim/user; CSTAR_EFFICIENCY_*)
E-4   Cf efficiency ramps 0.94 at 100 psia -> 0.97 at 250 psia, flat
      outside (same note as E-3). Over the burn eta_cf = 0.952-0.966.
      (vsim/user; CF_EFFICIENCY_*)
E-5   In the transient each efficiency is taken at the PREVIOUS step's Pc
      (0.05 s lag, < 0.1 % effect; done for solver robustness). The thrust
      targeter uses the curve at the exact Pc, with corners rounded over
      +-2 % of the ramp span. (mine; Hopper_Components.EnginePerformance)
E-6   Real chamber flow = ideal nozzle flow / eta_c*; real thrust = eta_cf x
      ideal thrust. All combustion and nozzle losses are in these two
      numbers. (vsim/mine)
E-7   The nozzle is ideal-gas, quasi-1D, isentropic, frozen at the chamber
      gamma and gas constant (no shifting equilibrium in the nozzle), with
      flow separation and shocks only as diagnostics. (FF IsentropicNozzle)
E-8   Pc is the nozzle stagnation pressure: no injector-face-to-throat
      (Rayleigh) pressure loss, no contraction-ratio effects. (mine)
E-9   The chamber has zero volume (no L*, no fill time, no combustion
      delay): Pc is whatever balances injector inflow and nozzle outflow
      at every instant. vehicle_sim instead relaxed Pc 30 % per 0.1 s.
      (mine)
E-10  Propellant ignites the moment it reaches the chamber (no ignition
      delay, no hard start); the torch only adds its propellant flow, it
      does not decide when the main chamber lights (see section 9a). (mine)
E-11  Chamber MR is computed from the actual ox and fuel flows and clipped
      to the map range 0.5-4.0. (mine)
E-12  Thrust is at sea-level ambient (14.8 psia) back pressure; no
      altitude compensation, no plume effects. (vsim)
E-13  No film cooling or other flow split; all propellant goes through the
      injector into the core. (mine)
E-14  Nominal Pc = 300 psia; used only to express "throttle %" in plots.
      (user; NOMINAL_CHAMBER_PRESSURE)
E-15  Nozzle-separation line on the exit-pressure plot at 0.4 x ambient
      (Summerfield-type rule of thumb from the old deck). (vsim;
      NOZZLE_SEPARATION_RATIO)

9. THRUST PROFILE, TARGETING AND SEQUENCE
=============================================================================
S-1   The flown thrust curve is vehicle_sim's 1dof profile (29.7 s,
      36 points, 890-1382 N), now in thrust_curves/1dof.csv. Not a fresh
      trajectory-sim export; kept on purpose (user, 2026-10-27) until Luca
      Zerega or Aniket Singh supply updated curves. (vsim; THRUST_CURVES)
S-2   Each profile point is held until the next ("step" interpolation, as
      vehicle_sim did); profile points closer than 0.1 s are resampled
      onto 0.1 s. A CSV is shifted so its first time is 0. (vsim/mine;
      PROP_PROFILE_INTERPOLATION, THRUST_CURVE_MIN_SPACING, Hopper_Inputs)
S-3   Flow commands come from a steady targeter: for each point, the Pc
      that gives the target thrust at MR 1.8 with the nominal engine, then
      total flow split at MR 1.8. The engine controller is therefore
      open-loop on flow: the transient tracks thrust only as well as the
      nominal engine model is right. (vsim/mine; Hopper_Thrust_Targeter)
S-4   Start sequence: OTV / FTV closed until 0.30 s, ramp linearly to the
      first profile point by 0.50 s; profile time 0 = sim time 0.50 s.
      vehicle_sim started at full flow at t = 0. (mine; MAIN_VALVE_OPEN_TIME,
      MAIN_VALVE_RAMP_TIME)
S-5   Shutdown: OTV / FTV ramp linearly to zero over 0.20 s after the last
      profile point, then 0.30 s of tail-off is simulated. (mine;
      TAIL_OFF_TIME)
S-6   The last profile point (29.7 s) has no hold time: shutdown starts the
      moment it is reached, so it is never judged. (vsim profile / mine)
S-7   OTV and FTV move together on the same schedule (no ox-lead or
      fuel-lead). (mine)
S-8   The torch fires for ~0.2 s (user). It is built (section 9a) but
      SWITCHED OFF by default (TORCH_ENABLED = False, per the boss: "don't
      worry about igniter for now"), so a normal run has no torch flow.

13. RUN REPORT DEFINITIONS (Hopper_Report.py)
=============================================================================
R-1   Burn time = span over which thrust >= 10 % of the maximum target
      thrust. (mine; REPORT_THRUST_ON_FRACTION)
R-2   Propellant residual = liquid left in the TANK at the end of the run
      (after the 0.3 s tail-off); line hold-up, sump/trapped propellant and
      unusable propellant are not separated out (see L-11). (mine)
R-3   Each profile point is judged at the last saved time inside its hold,
      before the next step starts. (mine)
R-4   Thrust must be within 2 % of target at every judged point. (mine;
      REPORT_THRUST_TOLERANCE)
R-5   A thrust level counts as reachable while OTV and FTV stay below
      99.9 % of max CdA. (mine)
R-6   Tank pressure min/max and injector stiffness are taken over the
      profile window only (start-up and shutdown excluded), except tank

15. SOLVER AND BACKEND (numerical assumptions)
=============================================================================
N-1   Implicit backward-Euler time integration, 0.05 s step, residual
      tolerance 1e-6, 2-point finite-difference Jacobian; a step that fails
      is retried at half the step. 0.05 s matches 0.01 s on engine results;
      tank pressure overshoots ~10 psi more. (mine; PROP_DT, PROP_RTOL,
      PROP_JACOBIAN_METHOD)
N-2   Through the opening and closing ramps the solver is forced to 0.01 s
      steps (breakpoints on the ramp) so ignition and shutdown converge.
      (mine; RAMP_BREAKPOINT_SPACING)
N-3   Mixture ratio uses a 1e-4 kg/s flow floor so it stays defined at zero
      flow ((ox + 1.8 x floor) / (fuel + floor)). (mine;
      MIXTURE_RATIO_FLOW_FLOOR)
N-4   Within 0.5 psi of ambient the nozzle flow is linear in (Pc - ambient)
      so the solver has a slope at zero flow. (mine;
      NOZZLE_LINEAR_PRESSURE_DROP; the LinearizedNozzle docstring still
      says 1 psi)
N-5   Throttle-valve balance: max((mdot - target) / 0.01 kg/s, (CdA - max
      CdA) / 1 mm2); tank surface balance in psi; targeter residual is
      thrust error / 100 N, accepted at 1e-4 (0.01 N). (mine)
N-6   Isp and delivered c* use a 1e-6 kg/s flow floor before ignition.
      (mine)
N-7   Liquid properties are memoised per (propellant, pressure,
      temperature) (consequence of F-3). (mine)
N-8   FullFlow junctions are zero-volume nodes (mass in = mass out every
      instant). (FF Volume with no volume)
N-9   Results are saved every 0.05 s (0.25 s in Monte Carlo runs); reports
      read the saved points only. (mine; PROP_SAVE_DT, MC_SAVE_DT)
N-10  Vent runs use a 0.1 s step up to 300 s; drain runs use 400 steps over
      2.5 x the constant-flow drain time (capped at 3600 s). (mine)
N-11  CavitatingVenturi defaults (only used if you add one): pressure
      recovery 0.85, cavitating Cd 0.94, non-cavitating Cd 0.6. (mine;
      Hopper_Elements)
