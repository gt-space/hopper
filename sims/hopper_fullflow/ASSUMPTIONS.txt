HOPPER FULLFLOW MODEL - KEY ASSUMPTIONS
=======================================
All inputs live in hopper_config.json; values known to be assumptions carry an EDIT
or GUESS note.


KEY INPUTS
----------
COPVs          2 x 9 L, 4503 psia, 293 K
Tanks          LOX 15.867 L, 16.00 kg, 90 K; RP-1 17.929 L, 13.00 kg, 293 K
               Both held at 500 psia +-5 psi by bang-bang solenoids
Collapse       LOX 2.0, fuel 1.1, constant over the burn
Pressurant     1/4" x 0.035" lines, 48 in; OR-1 7 mm2, OR-2 3 mm2; OBB / FBB
               18.68 mm2; check valves 12 mm2
Feed lines     3/8" x 0.01" wall SS; LOX 58 in (1.47 m), fuel 24 in (0.61 m)
Throttle       OTV / FTV max CdA 50 mm2, 180 lbf/s slew limit
Injector       OINJ 16.6 mm2, FINJ 15.0 mm2, regen 30 mm2
Regen heating  Fuel 293 K at regen inlet, 375 K at injector inlet
Engine         MR 1.25 at all throttle levels, throat 672 mm2, expansion 3.50
Efficiency     c* 0.80 -> 0.85 over 100-300 psia; Cf 0.94 -> 0.97 over
               100-250 psia
Thrust curve   GNC 52 m free flight, 29.5 s, 238-380 lbf
Purge          PR-REG 750 psig, 1.86 mm2 solenoids, 8.5 mm2 check valves,
               1 m of 1/4" x 0.035" tube


MODELLING SIMPLIFICATIONS
-------------------------
Propellants    Incompressible and single phase, with properties fixed at set
               pressure and loaded temperature, except fuel after regen. No
               cavitation or boil-off.
Pressurant     COPV and ullage gas are well mixed and adiabatic, which is
               conservative for COPV pressure. The identical bottles are one
               lumped volume. Each leg is lumped into one series CdA. Solenoids
               switch instantly, are checked every 0.05 s and start open.
Feed system    No fittings, bends or K-losses; gravity ignored. Lines start
               full, and line contents are not counted in the residuals.
Throttling     Valves are ideal flow controllers with no lag or leakage. Flow
               commands come from a steady targeter using the nominal engine,
               and both valves move together.
Regen          Fixed 375 K outlet at every throttle level and from the first
               instant, with no thermal lag. Jacket uses its inlet fuel
               properties; FINJ uses 375 K.
Engine         Zero-volume chamber, instant ignition, equilibrium combustion
               with fuel at 293 K (regen heat comes from the chamber gas), ideal
               frozen nozzle. All losses are in the two efficiencies. Constant
               14.8 psia ambient.
Off in a burn  Torch igniter is switched off. Vents, purges and the drain stay
               closed.


TORCH INPUTS AND INDEPENDENT COLD-GAS PURGE
-----------------------------------------
Fuel           FTV inlet -> FIMV -> FICK -> FIORF -> fuel injector -> Torch Chamber.
Purge          PR-REG outlet -> TFPV -> FIPCK -> FIPORF -> FIORF Outlet, then through
               the shared fuel injector and Torch Chamber. FIORF is before the tee.
Oxidizer       OTV inlet -> OIMV -> OICK -> OIORF -> OTHX -> ox injector -> Torch Chamber.
Outlet         One shared Torch Chamber volume -> Torch Throat -> Main Chamber.
OTHX           Tube with input length/diameter and prescribed Tout-Tin vs seconds
               since the torch valves begin opening. No phase-change model.
Status         torch.enabled is false: full vehicle/combustion coupling is pending.
Independent    Run isolated_sims/Hopper_Torch_Purge.py. Ideal cold nitrogen, gamma 1.4,
               293 K initially and in supply/main reservoirs. No combustion, residual
               feed flow, liquid displacement or trapped line gas. No valve cracking
               dP. Constant regulated source, no COPV depletion or regulator droop.
Sequence       Torch pressure imposed at 150 psia before 2 s. Main pressure steps
               14.8 -> 300 psia at 2 s; close FIMV/OIMV and open TFPV at 2 s.
               FIMV/TFPV travel 80 ms, OIMV 100 ms. Close TFPV at 7 s is an editable
               example. All actions are in torch.standalone_purge.sequence.events,
               using absolute simulation seconds; sequence.end_time_s ends the run.
               Old per-valve command_events_s cases remain replayable.
Chamber        After 2 s integrate mass and internal energy in 0.0019 L. Geometric
               bidirectional throat, no extra Cd; negative throat mdot is backflow
               from main into torch. Near-zero dP has configurable regularization.
Restrictions   Current user inputs: FIORF/OIORF 8 mm2, FIPORF 50 mm2, fuel injector
               0.8051 mm2. Each active purge restriction has its own solved pressure
               drop; no lumped allocation of drops. Earlier comparisons used a
               0.1051 mm2 injector; read saved input configs when comparing results.
Margin         Fully open equilibrium solves inlet mdot = throat mdot. Latest base
               inputs give about 15.693 psi margin and 9.502 g/s at 300 psia main;
               archived small-injector cases give 0.286 psi and 1.286 g/s. Throat is
               unchoked; this is not a demonstrated minimum design margin. The
               imposed initial pressure does not establish combustion performance.


SEPARATE CALCULATIONS
---------------------
Vent           Adiabatic from 505 psia to 1 psig, no LOX boil-off.
Drain          OFD only, with no ground-side plumbing, so times are optimistic.
Tank sweep     Steady state with tanks at set - 5 psi; valve design limit is
               80 % open.
Purge check    Steady flow from the end-of-burn COPV through an ideal regulator,
               compared by volume at feed-line pressure.
Monte Carlo    100 runs, uniform bands: CdA +-10 %, tank and COPV pressure
               +-2 %, loads +-1 %, collapse +-0.3, c* +-2 %, Cf +-1 %, COPV
               temperature +-10 K, MR +-3 %. The controller knows each run's
               true engine, which is optimistic.
